A few things you might wonder.

Do I need to work in tech or design?

Not at all. Sessions are open to curious people from any background. Each session explains who it’s for and any experience that would be helpful.

Why only 15 people?

Small groups make it easier to ask a question, share an experience, and remember the people you meet. The limit applies to attendees, with the host leading the session.

How do the breakout rooms work?

After the main conversation, peers continue discussing the topic together while the host takes short individual question slots. The agenda tells you how much time is available. Longer one-to-one sessions are booked separately.

Can I share my own experience as a host?

Yes. Hosts should have relevant experience, be part of the community, and enjoy helping people learn. Expertise matters, but so does being willing to listen.
